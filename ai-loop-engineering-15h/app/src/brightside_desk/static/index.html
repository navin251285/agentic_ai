Brightside support desk

Ask about an order, for example BS-1042, or ask for a sum or a refund. The desk looks things up with its tools, and it stops at its caps. A refund is only proposed: a checker approves it, and the payment service pays it once.

Scheduled runs and approvals

The heartbeat adds each ticket once, and a worker claims it with a lease. Each run works in its own git worktree, under a grant list of tools. A refund over the limit waits here for a person. Approve it by echoing the amount you were shown: the lease is checked again in the same step, so a late answer is refused.
