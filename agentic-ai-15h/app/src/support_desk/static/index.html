Brightside support desk

Two tools on one page. The order lookup answers from a tool; the refund desk cites the policy, remembers the customer and waits for a person before a refund is paid.

Order lookup

Ask about an order, such as "Where is my parcel for order 1002?". The agent looks it up with a tool and shows each tool call under its answer.

Refund desk

Customer C-88 has a lost parcel. Ask for the refund: the desk searches the policy, asks for the approval, and pays only after a person decides. Facts you save here are read at the start of each question.

Traces and cost
